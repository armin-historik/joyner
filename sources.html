Image and publication permissions

Permission to reproduce a photograph, scan or institutional image must be checked against the exact item and its licensing or written authorisation. A permission held by the researcher should be credited using the rights holder's required wording. The permission documents themselves are not published here.

Before adding archive images

Confirm the precise source institution, object identifier, authorised use, required credit and any restrictions. This avoids treating access to a digitised image as automatic permission to republish it.

Biographical and institutional sources

National Inventors Hall of Fame. “Marjorie Stewart Joyner,” inductee profile (2023). Birth and death dates, education, Walker company responsibilities, professional organisations and 2023 induction. Read the official profile.

National Archives Museum. “National Inventor’s Day: Marjorie S. Joyner,” online exhibit (5–28 February 2021). Chicago arrival, graduation, 1925 Walker school photograph, patent application and later advocacy. The 1925 image is credited to Chicago Public Library and is not treated as automatically reusable. Read the exhibition.

National Inventors Hall of Fame. “10 Things You Need to Know About Marjorie Stewart Joyner,” 2023 fact sheet. Useful leads for professional associations and later recognition; these require further archival corroboration. Read the fact sheet.

Research cautions

The pot-roast inspiration is a retrospective narrative, not a patent-file fact. References to a second patent do not establish its number. The 1928 patent case file is the primary evidence for the specific invention, but does not alone document commercial distribution. Third-party portrait rights remain unverified.

Additional verified primary and institutional sources

Joyner, Marjorie Stewart. Scalp Protector. U.S. Patent 1,716,173 (June 4, 1929).

Divisional application filed November 26, 1928; original application May 16, 1928. Named assignee: Madame C. J. Walker Manufacturing Company. Patent record · Original patent PDF. The full 1929 prosecution file has not been obtained.

Smithsonian National Museum of American History. Marjorie Stewart Joyner, American Enterprise / Black Main Street.

Exhibition interpretation of Walker franchises, beauty education, Black entrepreneurship and civil-rights organising. Exhibition page.

Smithsonian National Museum of American History. Making Waves: Beauty Salons and the Black Freedom Struggle.

Provides historical context and estimates for Joyner's education network. Museum essay.

Stanford Technology Law Review (2022). Centering Black Women Inventors.

Legal-historical discussion of Joyner's patent assignments, with further archival references. Scholarly article (PDF).

MIT Lemelson. Marjorie Joyner.

Secondary biography including the 1929 scalp protector and a reported death date of December 27, 1994. Profile.

Research and image-use cautions

Biographical references differ over Joyner's precise date of death; this site follows the December 27, 1994 date given by the National Inventors Hall of Fame and MIT Lemelson, while noting that some other accounts differ. Photographs and museum objects reproduced on third-party sites have not been copied into this website without verified reuse rights. Original patent drawings are separately attributed.
